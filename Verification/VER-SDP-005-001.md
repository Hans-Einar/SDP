  at verification time, but a later live rerun may differ. The corpus preserves
  the original capture identities and the fixed inclusive window.
- Private-repository links and evidence require authorized access. The
  authenticated verifier had access to all 35 owner repositories; public readers
  may not.
- This pass independently rechecked the complete inventory, all default heads,
  exact PR/check state, corpus structure, identity mapping, synthesis coverage,
  and representative repository claims. It did not independently reperform
  every product test, hardware/physical experiment, historical agent session,
  or external primary-source claim cited across all 17 reports.
- Local-link validation proves referenced repository paths exist. It does not
  prove that every external URL remains available or that every external claim
  is semantically correct.
- Most studied repositories expose agent/reviewer/owner activity through the
  single GitHub identity `Hans-Einar`. Fresh-context and role-independence claims
  cannot be proven solely from GitHub actor identity.
- The two successful Actions jobs uploaded no artifacts. Their immutable run
  and job URLs, conclusions, exact head, and logs remain available through
  GitHub.
- The shared verification run began clean. After all evidence-bearing
  exact-candidate checks completed, concurrent review/rework activity created
  `CodeReview/REV-SDP-005-001.md` and began modifying the shared working tree
  while HEAD remained at the verified candidate. Those uncommitted changes are
