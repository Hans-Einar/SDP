# Sprints

A Sprint optionally groups selected plans and/or direct KanBan cards under one
goal. Use one concise record with SPR-SDP-NNNN identity, source references,
Plans and Members snapshots, state, acceptance and final dispositions. Plans and
cards remain in their own locations with reciprocal SprintId metadata; do not
copy their text here. A completed planning Study stays a source reference.

Use [Plans](../ProjectManagement/Plans.md) and the
[management contract](../ProjectManagement/README.md) for selection, start,
membership changes and closure. Start selected plans and activate their execution
cards when work begins. Explicitly remove deferred work before closure, preserving
its identity and history; an all-deferred Sprint may then close empty. Independent
plans/cards and direct Scrum → Maintenance remain valid. Do not invent a Sprint
for completed work solely to fill a hierarchy.

Existing Sprint records retain their dated scope and identifiers. New work need
not copy their richer iteration/notes hierarchy. System design/code changes go
to Traceability with management references. The selected delivery in
[SPR-SDP-0001](Sprint--%230001--SDPTool-preview-and-navigation.md) is complete.
