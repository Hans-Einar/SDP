## Selected presentation and semantics

Use two separate projections: all Activities with explicit refines arrows
(detailed activity to abstract activity), and a separate dependency diagram with
depends-on arrows (dependent to prerequisite). The dependency graph appears only
when such facts exist; it also includes isolated activities. Neither relation
implies inheritance, duration, a calendar schedule or execution order. A mixed
graph or synthetic Gantt would blur decomposition and prerequisites or invent dates.

A source-linked activity register records immediate refinement parents and explicit
implementation-status. Unspecified remains unspecified; planned/implemented/verified
are source assertions, never test evidence or owner acceptance. Status does not
propagate between parents/children or across dependencies.

Companion tables show addresses Functionality with its explicit owner (owns),
delivers Feature and illustrates Scenario. These are modeled coverage/commitments,
not proof of feature completion or scenario execution. Keep exact proof IDs and
original declaration links; do not infer new milestones from Features, source
files or KanBan. Include all Functionality without an addresses relation as a
model-coverage inventory; this does not assert unimplemented native code.

Keep diagrams focused on Activities: owners/functions/features/scenarios belong
in linked tables, avoiding one large mixed graph. Retain all activities, including
isolated and statusless declarations. Empty inventories/dependencies/commitments
are explicit. Actual XFMD has no Activities and 28 unaddressed Functionality
declarations; show that truth without inventing a work plan.
