## Active core and proposal separation

[SDL structural core](../../../SDL/docs/profiles/SDL-Structural-Core-Profile.md)
owns sections 1–11 and registered additions. [Candidates](../../../SDL/docs/studies/SDL-Language-Candidates.md)
own sections 12–16. The old address contains only routing links. All 15 original
fenced grammar/example blocks were preserved; four complete profile examples
validate through the real SDL CLI. Corrected the duplicate 10.3 subsection label
to 10.4, retaining the old address in the routing map. No grammar was adopted or
removed. The source split manifest records provenance.

The [SDUI map](../../../SDUI/docs/README.md) classifies every one of its 17 subject
documents into current contract/implementation and dated background. Research
profiles remain dated comparative studies. Go evidence records observed milestone
results, not promises about current external repositories. Generated reports remain
tool-owned; source assertions do not become observed execution evidence.

## Process harmonization

How-SDP-Works describes the current distributed 01–07 profile. The R3
[phase proposal](../../../docs/process/Project-Phase-Profile-Proposal.md) is the
single reviewable five-directory replacement candidate, with per-phase studies.
The [discovery/extraction proposal](../../../docs/process/Project-Discovery-and-Extraction-Proposal.md)
is a separate tool contract, not installed release metadata. Template and Toolkit
entry points explicitly preserve that distinction. No installer destination or
language keyword changed. Deeper feature-governance and SDP 2.0 studies remain
labeled proposals, not competing active directory contracts.

## Closure boundary

R1 physical migration, R2 status corrections, L1 English translation and R3
core/proposal/authority separation are delivered. The owner can review these
results in KB-SDP-010. New grammar, a chosen future process profile and its
installer implementation have separate owners/cards; this consolidation does
not claim to settle all open research questions or implement those proposals.
