# Reading study and selected outcome

The authoritative owner study and NavigationDesign remain in the collaborating
HEOS repository:

- [Study](../../../../../HEOS/SDP/03--Architecture/SDLBrowsing/Study.md).
- [NavigationDesign](../../../../../HEOS/SDP/03--Architecture/SDLBrowsing/NavigationDesign.md).

The observed problem is information overload: duplicated semantic/diagram routes,
unreadable proof inventories, unowned fragments and missing collection pages make
system understanding difficult despite rich model facts. The selected solution
moves semantic organization and reading documents into SDL/SDPTool; XFMD validates
and consumes final actions, labels, ordering and renderable resources generically.

[DesignPlan](../../../04--Design/SDPTool/SDLBrowsing/Plan.md) records the design route.
[ImplementationContract](../04-Design/ImplementationContract.md) is the executable
handoff. [Verification](../06-Verification/Verification.md) records the tested
outcome, including the rejected dense diagram approach and qualified partitioned
channel/mode pages. Requirements and SDL models retain their canonical homes.
